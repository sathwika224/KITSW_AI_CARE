KITSW AI CARE - EMAIL NOTIFICATION SETUP
========================================

The application is configured to read SMTP settings from a local .env file.
The complaint notification workflow works in-app even before SMTP credentials are supplied.

1. Copy .env.example to .env

2. For Gmail, edit .env:

KITSW_SMTP_HOST=smtp.gmail.com
KITSW_SMTP_PORT=587
KITSW_SMTP_USER=YOUR_GMAIL_ADDRESS
KITSW_SMTP_PASSWORD=YOUR_GOOGLE_APP_PASSWORD
KITSW_FROM_EMAIL=YOUR_GMAIL_ADDRESS

Use a Google App Password, NOT your normal Gmail password.

3. Start the app:

python -m streamlit run app.py

4. Submit a complaint or change a complaint status.

The application will:
- save the complaint/status first;
- create the in-app notification;
- attempt the real email through SMTP;
- record the delivery result in data/email_notifications.csv.

Security:
- Do not commit .env to GitHub.
- Do not share your App Password.
